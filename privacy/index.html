Hand Knee Foot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Hand Knee Foot (“the app”) is a card game for iPhone and iPad. Offline play stays free and local. Optional Online play is a paid add-on.

Data collection

The developer does not collect or receive personal data and operates no servers that receive it. The app does not use analytics, ads, trackers, or crash reporters that send data off-device. It has no app-specific account; an Apple iCloud account is required for the planned sync feature.

On-device storage

Game progress, preferences, and local history are stored on your device. iCloud sync is planned and is not yet available in the current app. When it ships and is enabled, it will copy the in-progress game, completed-game history and Stats, New Game preferences, and table settings to your private iCloud database. Developer preferences are never included. Local data remains on your device.

Network access

The current app works entirely offline and does not need a network connection to play. The following is the approved policy for the planned iCloud sync release; iCloud sync is not yet available in the current app. When it ships, sync will default on when you are signed in to iCloud; turning it off will stop the app’s iCloud traffic and keep local data. An Apple iCloud account is needed for sync.

Works entirely offline. When iCloud sync is enabled, your games and settings sync through your private iCloud database. Nothing is sent to servers operated by us. Turning sync off stops the app’s iCloud traffic and keeps local data.

Online play

If you choose Online play, you sign in with Apple (no name or email is requested) and the online service stores only what it needs to run the table:

  • an opaque Apple user ID (Sign in with Apple sub)
  • a themed handle you pick from suggested words
  • device records and push notification tokens (so we can notify you about your turn)
  • session token hashes (not the raw session token)
  • purchase / entitlement records (so access stays in sync with Apple)
  • game records and results

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, advertising identifier, analytics, or tracking data. We do not sell personal information.

Online data is hosted on Railway in the United States (US West region). Sign-in, purchases, and push delivery also use Apple. Encrypted database backups are kept in an off-site object store for about 30 days.

Retention:

  • Sessions expire 180 days after last use (or sooner if you sign out or delete the account).
  • Game results are kept until you delete your online account.
  • Backups are retained for 30 days.

You can delete your online account from inside the app. Deletion revokes the Apple token for this app, removes your personal rows (handle, devices, sessions, entitlements), turns unfinished seats into computer-held Left seats, and anonymises your results as “Deleted player.” Deleting the account does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage or cancel that in Apple’s subscription settings.

Children’s privacy

The app is intended for a general audience (expected App Store age rating 4+) and does not target children with ads or personalized content. Offline play does not collect personal information. Online play stores only the limited account data listed above and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. The date above will reflect the current version.
